# GDP-By-Region
Analyzing GDP, Inflation, and the Unemployment Rate by Regions of the United States.  
This is an analysis, in python, of what the difference is between the different areas of the United States (South, Northeast, West, and Midwest) in terms of GDP, inflation, and the unemployment rate.  The data is imported, cleaned, and then put into a SQL database from which visualizations are drawn and inferences made.
